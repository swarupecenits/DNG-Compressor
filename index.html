    How it works

    1. 1

      Pick your ProRAW files

      From the Files app on iPhone, or drag them in on a computer. Several at once is fine.

    2. 2

      Squeezed on your device

      Each tile of the raw image is re-encoded with lossless JPEG XL, then decoded again to prove every value matches the original.

    3. 3

      Save the smaller copy

      Previews, metadata and masks are carried over untouched. Your original files are never changed.

    Questions

    Is it really lossless?

    Yes. ProRAW stores its raw image with lossless JPEG, an old and fairly inefficient format. DNG Squeeze decodes each tile, encodes it again with lossless JPEG XL (allowed since DNG 1.7), then decodes the new tile and checks that every value matches the original exactly before using it. Everything else in the file — previews, metadata and semantic masks — is copied byte for byte.

    How much smaller will my photos get?

    Usually about a quarter. A 12 MP iPhone 12 Pro photo went from 29.2 MB to 21.6 MB (−26%) on Balanced. Detailed or noisy photos shrink a little less, simple ones more.

    Which apps can open the compressed files?

    Apps that support DNG 1.7 with JPEG XL, such as Apple Photos and Preview on iOS 18 and macOS 15 or later, and current versions of Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw. Older apps may refuse the file or only show its built-in preview, so check that your copies open where you need them before deleting any originals.

    How do I use it on iPhone?

    In Photos, open a ProRAW photo, tap Share and choose Save to Files. Then tap Choose DNG files here, pick Choose File (or Browse) and select them. Picking straight from the photo library can hand over a JPEG instead of the DNG. When a photo is done, tap Save and choose Save to Files. Keep the screen on while it works — iOS pauses pages in the background.

    Does it work offline? Can I install it?

    Yes. After your first visit everything is stored on your device, so it also works without a connection. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome or Edge, use Install app.

    Which files are supported?

    Apple ProRAW DNGs, and other “linear” DNGs whose raw image is compressed with lossless JPEG. Ordinary camera raw files with a Bayer mosaic can’t use JPEG XL under the DNG rules, so they are left alone, and files that already use JPEG XL are skipped.

    Is it free? What happens to my photos?

    It’s completely free: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your photos are processed by WebAssembly inside your browser and are never uploaded anywhere.

    Can my iPhone shoot smaller ProRAW files directly?

    iPhone 16 Pro and later can save ProRAW as lossless JPEG XL straight away — look for the ProRAW format option in Settings → Camera → Formats. DNG Squeeze is for older iPhones and for the photos you already have.
